Where migratory birds are through the year

       

      Bands show how far north or south each population sits. Wider means more birds.

      About this map

      The coloured glow is each species' estimated relative abundance for every week of the year, from eBird Status and Trends at the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. Their models combine millions of eBird checklists with satellite land-cover data. Brighter means more birds relative to that species' own range, so colours can't be compared between species.

      The lines on the globe follow the weighted centre of each regional population, week by week. Populations are separated by longitude into the Americas, Europe and Africa, and Asia and the Pacific. A centre is an average: birds spread across a wide area can have a centre where few birds actually are, especially at sea.

      Data are estimates for one year (2023) and are not live sightings.
